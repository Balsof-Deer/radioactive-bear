DangerBear is an app that helps students find, add and review places to study, work, unwind and meet. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

DangerBear is currently a **test version**. Features may change, and test data may be reset.

## Who is responsible for your information

DangerBear is run by Pholoso Lekagane. For any question or request about your information, email **plekagane@gmail.com**.

## Who can use DangerBear

You must be **18 or older** to create an account.

## What we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account details:** your email address and password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.
- **Profile:** a display name and a profile photo, if you choose to add them, and the amenities you prefer (such as wifi or quiet spaces).
- **Places you add:** the name, description, address, map position and amenity tags of each location you submit, and any photos you upload for it.
- **Reviews:** your ratings (overall and per category, such as noise level or wifi) and anything you write.

### Information collected when you use the app

- **Your device's location**, while the app is open and only if you allow it. It's used to show nearby places, centre the map on you, and help place a new location on the map. To find nearby places, your current position is sent to our database as part of the search. It isn't saved to your account or profile.
- **Dates and times** of when you created your account and added content.
- **Settings stored only on your phone:** your light or dark mode choice, whether "Match my preferences" is on, and your sign-in session. The sign-in session is stored encrypted.

### What we don't collect

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell your information.

## Photo metadata

Photos taken on phones can contain hidden details, such as the exact place the photo was taken. Before JPEG and PNG photos are uploaded, the app removes this information. Other photo formats aren't accepted, except WebP, which is uncommon on Android. WebP photos are uploaded as they are.

## How we use your information

- To create and secure your account, and to confirm your email address with a one-time code.
- To show you places near you and let you add, review and photograph them.
- To sort places that match your preferred amenities to the top when you ask for that.
- To let administrators review new places and remove content that breaks the rules.
- To keep the service working and secure, including preventing spam (for example, limiting how many places one person can add in a day).

## Who can see your information

| Information | You | Other signed-in users | Administrators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | Yes | No | Yes |
| Display name | Yes | No, your reviews appear as "A student" | Yes |
| Profile photo | Yes | No | Yes |
| Your reviews (ratings and text) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Places you add, and their photos | Yes | Yes, once they pass the review stage | Yes |
| Your device location | Only on your phone | No | No |

The app is only available to signed-in users. Nothing is published on the open web.

## Services we use

We use these providers to run DangerBear. They process information only to provide their service:

- **Supabase:** accounts and sign-in, the database and photo storage. It creates the sign-up code emails and hands them to Gmail to deliver. Our data is hosted in [SUPABASE PROJECT REGION, e.g. "Europe (Frankfurt)"; see the Supabase dashboard > Project settings].
- **Google (Gmail):** delivers the sign-up code emails.
- **OpenFreeMap:** supplies the map. Like any website, their servers receive your device's internet address and which part of the map is being shown.
- **Google (Android):** your phone's location services provide your position to the app.

Some of these providers may store information outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on the provider's own safeguards for international transfers.

## How long we keep information

- **While your account exists,** we keep your account, profile and content.
- **When you delete your account** (Profile > Delete account), your account, email address, display name, profile photo and preferences are deleted. Reviews, places and place photos you added stay in the app so other students can still use them, but they are anonymised and shown as from a "Deleted user".
- Deleted information may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time before it is overwritten.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and change your information:** you can edit your display name, photo and preferences in Profile.
- **Delete your account:** at any time, in Profile > Delete account.
- **Other requests:** to ask for a copy of your information, correct it, or object to how it's used, email plekagane@gmail.com.
- **Location:** you can turn off location access in your phone's settings. The map and nearby list won't work without it, but the rest of the app still does.

If you are in South Africa, the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you these rights, and you may complain to the Information Regulator ([www.inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za)). If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you similar rights.

## How we protect your information

- All communication with our servers is encrypted.
- Database access rules limit who can read or change each piece of information.
- Your sign-in session is encrypted on your phone.
- Passwords must meet strength requirements.
- Photo location data is removed before upload.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as required by law.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.
